## Overview

Behind every successful innovation is collaboration. Yet millions of students, researchers, and innovators are stuck working in isolation. ResearchBuddy solves this by providing:

- Intelligent AI matchmaking based on research intent and expertise
- Dedicated collaboration workspaces
- Verified researcher identities
- A global network of interdisciplinary innovators

Current statistics:
- 2,400+ researchers registered
- 870+ active collaborations
- 60+ research domains covered
- Global cross-border matching

## Problem Statement

Thousands of research ideas fail before they begin. Common challenges include:

| Challenge | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Thesis Students | Cannot find committed teammates or willing supervisors for their first research project |
| Interdisciplinary Researchers | AI researcher needs a climate expert, healthcare innovator needs data scientists - no way to find them |
| Broken Platforms | LinkedIn and ResearchGate are built for corporate networking, not intelligent collaboration matchmaking |
| Ideas Lost | Thousands of research ideas die in ideation phase because the right collaborators never connect |

## How It Works

1. **Build Your Researcher Profile**
   - Add academic background, technical expertise, publications, research interests, collaboration goals, and availability

2. **AI Matchmaking Engine Activates**
   - System analyzes your profile using semantic understanding, embeddings, and similarity analysis
   - Compares research domains, skills, and collaboration behavior to generate best-fit matches

3. **Precision-Matched Connections**
   - Goes beyond keywords to find complementary collaborators
   - Example: AI engineer exploring climate modeling matched with the right environmental scientist anywhere in the world

4. **Collaborate & Execute**
   - Move from matching to execution with dedicated workspaces
   - Share datasets, track milestones, and co-author papers in real time
   - No more ghosting, no more isolation
